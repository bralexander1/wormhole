ABOUT BASH

Born in 1994 in Annapolis, Maryland in the morning of a cold and frosty February day, born and raised in the marshes and swamp gas of the Chesapeake Bay, BASH was born to a Virgina man and a Severna Park mama. In 1995 they claimed their stake on Kent Island, a small peice of land on Maryland's Eastern Shore, connected to Central Maryland by the Chesapeake Bay Bridge.

BASH has a wide range of passions and interests than span many hobbies, activities, and subjects from music, art and photography to Star Wars Odd World and the Lord of the Rings
